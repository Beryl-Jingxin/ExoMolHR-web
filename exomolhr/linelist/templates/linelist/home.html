Welcome to ExoMolHR

Empirical High-Resolution Molecular Spectrum Database and Calculator

ExoMolHR is an advanced platform delivering empirically refined high-resolution spectral data. Built upon the rigorous MARVEL procedure and the comprehensive ExoMol project, we provide precise line positions and computed intensities specifically curated and tailored for your high-resolution research needs.

High Accuracy

Empirical energy levels with rigorous uncertainty quantification ensuring top-tier spectral fidelity.

Dynamic Calculation

Generate spectra on-the-fly for custom wavenumber/wavelength ranges, thermodynamic temperatures, and intensity thresholds.

Online Spectrum Plotting

Interactively visualize and plot stick spectra for mixed molecules and isotopologues directly in your browser.

Seamless Integration

Direct data export and seamless compatibility with the PyExoCross package for comprehensive offline analysis.
